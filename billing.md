# Billing

You pay per Google Ads account, each month, by card through Stripe. Every account costs $149 a month, up to 10 accounts on 1 plan. There is one plan with every tool in it, and you can cancel anytime.

## Prices

| What | Price |
|---|---|
| Each Google Ads account | $149 a month |
| Most accounts on 1 plan | 10 |
| Extra AI credits (optional) | $25 for 1,000 credits |

**Example:** 3 accounts cost 3 x $149 = $447 a month.

## What each account gets every month

| Tool | Allowance per account, per month |
|---|---|
| Google Ads audit | Included |
| AI credits | 1,000 |
| Campaign builds | 3 |
| Search Optimizer runs | 5 |
| AI ad suggestions | 20 |
| Performance Max Optimizer runs | 8 |
| Product titles written by AI | 1,000 |
| Products with AI-filled details | 300 |

AI credits pay for AI work, like ad text, campaign checks, product titles and questions in the chat. The tools above use credits and also have their own monthly limit.

Allowances and monthly credits reset every month. Unused monthly credits don't carry over. PerfoAds' daily account checks are included and do not count.

See how much you have used in **Settings**, on the **Account** tab. If a tool reaches its limit, the app tells you, and that tool stops until your allowance resets. If an account runs out of AI credits, you can buy 1,000 more for $25. Credits you buy never expire and are used after the monthly ones.

## Pay, change or cancel

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Account**.
2. Click **Subscription**. This opens Stripe's secure billing page.
3. There you manage your card, your invoices and your plan.

You can also click the **PRO** badge next to your name at the bottom of the menu to open your plan page.

- If you cancel, your plan runs until the end of the period you paid for.
- If a payment fails, access stops until the invoice is paid.

## Card details

You type your card details on Stripe's page. PerfoAds never sees your full card number.
