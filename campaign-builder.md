# Campaign Builder

The Campaign Builder creates a new Google Ads campaign for you, step by step, from your website. You check everything before it is created.

## How to start

In the menu, click **Campaign Builder**, then **New Campaign**. You can also click **New campaign** on the [Overview](/viewing-performance.html) when 1 account is picked.

A short chat asks a few questions first: the campaign type (Search or Performance Max), your website, where the ads should show and what they should promote.

## Search campaign

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Account | Pick the Google Ads account. |
| Brief | Tell PerfoAds about your business and goal. |
| Strategy | PerfoAds reads your website. You check what it found and answer a few questions about your offer. |
| Keywords | Keyword ideas with search volumes. They come from your account's past searches, from Google's Keyword Planner, or both. Keywords you already run are skipped. |
| Structure | Ad groups, match types, bids, daily budget and negative keywords. You also get advice on which bidding strategy to use. |
| Ad Copy | Headlines and descriptions, plus sitelinks, callouts and structured snippets. Rewrite any line you do not like. |
| Deploy | A full preview of everything that will be created. |

## Performance Max campaign

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Account | Pick the Google Ads account. |
| Brief | Tell PerfoAds about your business and goal. |
| Strategy | PerfoAds reads your website. |
| Themes | Search themes. They tell Google which searches matter to you. |
| Settings | Campaign name, daily budget and bidding. |
| Assets | Headlines, descriptions and images for the asset group. |
| Deploy | A full preview of everything that will be created. |

## Before anything is created

- The Deploy step lists every item that will be created. Nothing is sent to Google Ads until you confirm.
- New campaigns are created **paused**. They do not spend money until you turn them on in Google Ads or in PerfoAds.
- You can undo a created campaign for 30 days from the account history. See [Changes and undo](/changes-and-undo.html).

## Other ways to use your campaign

On a Search campaign's Deploy step, you can also download a file for Google Ads Editor and upload it yourself.

## Drafts and history

Your work is saved as a draft as you go. Find your drafts and past builds under **Campaign Builder**, then **History**.

## Monthly allowance

Your plan includes 3 campaign builds per account each month. See [Billing](/billing.html).
