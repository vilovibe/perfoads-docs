# Performance Max Optimizer

Performance Max hides a lot of what it does. This workspace shows it: your assets, your audience signals, the searches you appear for and where your money goes. The AI suggests fixes, and you approve each one before anything changes.

## How to open it

Go to the [Overview](/viewing-performance.html). Click the sparkle next to a Performance Max campaign.

## The tabs

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| Recommendations | Your to-do list for this campaign. |
| Asset Groups | Check each asset group against Google's minimums and fill the gaps. |
| Text Assets | Every headline and description, with Google's rating for each. |
| Extensions | Sitelinks, callouts and snippets for this campaign. |
| Signals | Audience signals and search themes for each asset group. |
| Search Insights | The search topics Google matched your campaign to. |
| Breakdowns | Results split in different ways. |
| Products | Only for shop campaigns. How each product performs. |
| Channels | How your budget splits across Google's networks. |
| Placements | Where your ads were shown outside Google search. You can exclude bad ones. |
| Settings | Status, budget, bidding, locations and languages. |
| History | Every change made here, with undo. |

## Analyze

Click **Analyze** to run 1 deep AI check of the whole campaign. Each tab then shows its part of the result.

- It usually takes under a minute.
- The result is saved, so opening the campaign again costs nothing.
- Click **Re-analyze** to refresh it.

## Recommendations

1 list of everything worth doing: items from your audit, from the daily checks and from the AI analysis. Items with a **Fix** button take you straight to the right tab.

## Asset Groups

For each asset group you see how many headlines, long headlines, descriptions, images and videos it has, compared with what Google asks for. You also see spend, conversions, return on ad spend and more.

- Pause or turn on an asset group.
- Click **Fix** to let the AI draft the missing assets. You review them before anything is sent.

## Text Assets

Replace weak headlines and descriptions with AI suggestions, then click **Deploy changes**. You see a preview first.

## Signals

Signals tell Google who your buyers are. They guide Google, they do not limit it.

- Add audiences from your Google Ads audience lists.
- Let the AI write search themes for asset groups that have few.

## Search Insights

The search categories Google showed your ads for, with clicks, conversions and value for each. You can add a bad category as a negative.

## Safety

Every change goes through a preview. You confirm it before it reaches Google Ads. Most changes can be undone for 30 days. See [Changes and undo](/changes-and-undo.html).
