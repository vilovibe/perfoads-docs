# Data and privacy

This page explains in plain words what PerfoAds can see, what it does with it, and how you stay in control. The legal details are in our [privacy policy](https://perfoads.com/privacypolicy).

## Signing in

- You sign in with Google. PerfoAds never sees your Google password.
- You stay signed in for 7 days. Then you sign in again.
- **Sign Out** is in **Settings**, on the **Account** tab.

## What PerfoAds can see

- **Your Google Ads account:** campaigns, ads, keywords, search terms, budgets, conversions and results.
- **Your Merchant Center account:** only if you use the [Feed Optimizer](/feed-optimizer.html). Your products and how they are set up.
- **Your email address**, from your Google login.
- **Your answers** to the questions when you start an audit or build a campaign, and what PerfoAds reads on your website.

## What PerfoAds does with it

- Shows it to you on the Overview and in the tools.
- Checks it for problems and writes findings and suggestions.
- Sends parts of it to an AI service to write those findings and suggestions.

## What PerfoAds can change

PerfoAds can change your Google Ads and Merchant Center accounts, but only:

- after the account owner turns on **Google Ads writes**, and
- after you see a preview and approve each change.

Most changes can be undone for 30 days. See [Changes and undo](/changes-and-undo.html).

## Who else can see your data

- **Your team:** people you invite. Specialists only see the accounts you assign to them.
- **People with a share link:** if you share an audit report, anyone with that link can read that 1 report. You can turn the link off at any time.
- **Stripe:** handles your payments. You type your card details on Stripe's page, not ours.

## Removing an account

On the [Overview](/viewing-performance.html), open the account's menu and choose **Remove Client**. This deletes that account's Overview, monitoring and optimizer data straight away. It cannot be undone. Your audit reports stay in your history.

## Taking back access

Remove PerfoAds on your Google account's [third-party access page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). PerfoAds then can no longer read or change your accounts.

## Deleting your data or asking what we hold

Email [privacy@perfoads.com](mailto:privacy@perfoads.com). Your rights and how we handle requests are in the [privacy policy](https://perfoads.com/privacypolicy).

## Emails from PerfoAds

The app does not send you email notifications today.
