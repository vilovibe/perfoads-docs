Billing

You pay per Google Ads account, each month, by card through Stripe. The first account is $59 a month. Each extra account is $39 a month, up to 10 accounts.

Prices

What Price
First account $59 a month
Each extra account $39 a month
Most accounts on 1 plan 10
1 audit on its own, no plan $19.99, paid once

Example: 3 accounts cost $59 + $39 + $39 = $137 a month.

The single audit gives you 1 audit report. The monthly tools, like the optimizers and making changes to Google Ads, need a monthly plan. You can buy the single audit on the single audit page.

Tool Allowance per account, per month
Google Ads audits 2

Performance Max Optimizer runs 8
Performance Max deep analyses 3
Product titles written by AI 1,000
Custom label assignments 300

Allowances reset every month. PerfoAds' daily account checks are included and do not count.

See how much you have used in Settings, on the Account tab. If you run out, the app tells you, and that tool stops until your allowance resets.

Pay, change or cancel

  1. Go to Settings, then Account.
  2. Click Subscription. This opens Stripe's secure billing page.
  3. There you manage your card, your invoices and your plan.

You can also click the PRO badge next to your name at the bottom of the menu to open your plan page.

  • If you cancel, your plan runs until the end of the period you paid for.
  • If a payment fails, access stops until the invoice is paid.

Card details

You type your card details on Stripe's page. PerfoAds never sees your full card number.
